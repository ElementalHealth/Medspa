About BWA

Born from the ER.
Built for Longevity.

Brookhaven Wellness + Aesthetics was founded by a team of Emergency Medicine physicians who spent careers making high-stakes decisions under pressure — and realized that the same clinical precision applied to aesthetic and preventive medicine produces a fundamentally different result.

We are opening in the heart of Brookhaven in Q4 2026, in a purpose-built 1,865 sq ft space designed to feel less like a medical office and more like the private health club you've always wanted. Our IV lounge, four dedicated procedure rooms, and private consultation suite are designed around your comfort and privacy.

We're not trying to be the biggest medspa in Atlanta. We're building the most trusted one — where the people treating you have the training to handle anything, and the taste to make it beautiful.
